
# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce trends using an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into employee turnover based on age, education, salary, years at the company, job role, gender, and department. 

It helps HR teams understand attrition patterns and identify areas that need attention.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employee Attrition: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Average Employee Age: 37
- Average Salary: 6.50K
- Average Years at Company: Verify the displayed KPI against the underlying measure.

## Key Insights
1. Employees aged 26–35 represent the largest attrition group, with 116 employees.
2. Life Sciences accounts for the largest share of attrition by education (38%), followed by Medical (27%).
3. Employees earning up to 5K account for 163 departures, the highest among salary groups.
4. Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition count (62), followed by Sales Executives (57).
5. Employee departures are highest during the first year at the company, reaching 59 employees.
